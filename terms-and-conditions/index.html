Work is booked when you accept the written quote by email, message or other agreed confirmation. We schedule installation or repair dates after acceptance and subject to weather, material availability and existing commitments.

We install materials as specified in the accepted quote — for example pressure-treated timber closeboard or feather edge, composite panels, gravel boards, concrete posts, or metal systems for commercial work. Substitutions of equivalent specification may be used if the stated product is unavailable, with your agreement.

5. Payment

  • Payment terms are set out on your written quote (for example deposit before materials are ordered, balance on completion).
  • Invoices are payable within the period stated on the invoice unless otherwise agreed.
  • We may pause or stop work if agreed payments are not received when due.

6. Changes to scope

Additional work requested after acceptance — extra fence length, unforeseen ground preparation, removal of more old fencing than quoted — will be priced separately and confirmed before we carry it out.

If you cancel after materials have been ordered or a start date reserved, reasonable costs already incurred may be charged as stated in your quote or invoice. We will try to agree a revised date if you need to postpone; repeated postponements may affect scheduling priority.
