Wooden Gates Birmingham

Home/Wooden Gates Birmingham

Wooden gates provide pedestrian access through a fence line — side passage gates between terraced houses, garden gates to rear plots on sloping sites, or vehicle gates where width allows. The gate frame must suit hinge load and the post must be adequately sized to prevent sag on clay soil.

Gate specification

Gates are built or supplied to opening width and height. Hinges, latches and drop bolts are specified in the quote. Alignment with existing garden fencing board profile keeps a consistent appearance.

Gate repair — re-hanging, new frame, latch replacement — is listed under fence repairs when only the gate needs attention.

Areas we work in

Wooden gates are supplied and hung across Birmingham including Shirley, Solihull, Edgbaston and Bournville. See areas we cover for postcodes served.

Frequently asked questions

Can a new gate match my fence?expand_more

Board profile, height and timber treatment can be matched to an existing run when specifications are known or sampled on site.

What width can a side gate be?expand_more

Width is measured between posts or walls. Narrow terraced side access in areas such as Selly Oak and Sparkhill often limits width to standard pedestrian sizes — exact measurement is taken before ordering.

Can you re-hang a sagging gate?expand_more

Yes, when the post remains sound. Re-hanging, new hinges or a replacement frame are quoted under fence repairs if the gate alone needs attention.

Are drop bolts included?expand_more

Drop bolts, latches and hinges are specified in the written quote. Security requirements on rear alleys in Aston and Witton are noted when measuring.
