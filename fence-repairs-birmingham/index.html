Fence Repairs Birmingham

Home/Fence Repairs Birmingham

Fence repairs address localised failure — a broken panel after wind across exposed hillsides, a post that has worked loose in clay or Mercia mudstone soil, rot at the base of a gravel board, or gate hinges that no longer align. Repair is practical when the post line remains straight and rot has not spread through multiple bays.

Panel and board replacement

Single closeboard boards, feather edge panels or lap panels can be swapped without replacing an entire run when posts and rails are sound.

  • check_circleMatch existing profile where possible
  • check_circleReuse sound posts
  • check_circleReplace fixings with galvanised screws

Post and base repairs

Leaning posts are re-set in concrete or replaced if the timber is rotted at ground level. This work disturbs less of the garden than a full boundary replacement.

  • check_circleConcrete footing depth to panel height
  • check_circleTemporary support during cure
  • check_circleRealignment of rails

Repair or replace?

Repair suits isolated damage: one or two panels, a single post, or gate hardware. Replacement is more practical when multiple posts show rot at ground level, the line has fallen out of alignment on a sloping boundary, or you want to change height or material.

We describe both options in the quote so you can compare cost against expected lifespan of the remaining structure.

Typical repair requests in Birmingham

Wind damage is common across exposed rear gardens on higher ground in Moseley, Selly Oak and Northfield. Rot at gravel board level appears where moisture sits against clay soil on terraced plots. Side gate sagging often results from hinge wear or post movement in narrow passages between Victorian terraces in Bournville and Sparkhill.

Frequently asked questions

Can you repair one panel only?expand_more

Yes, when adjacent posts and rails are structurally sound and a matching panel or board profile is available.

How quickly can repair work be scheduled?expand_more

Scheduling depends on current workload and material availability. Urgent security gaps are prioritised when described at enquiry.

Do you repair metal or security fencing?expand_more

We handle timber garden fencing and gates routinely. Security and commercial metal fencing repairs are assessed case by case — describe the system when enquiring.

What should I send when requesting a repair quote?expand_more

Photos of the damage, your postcode, and whether the fence is closeboard, feather edge or panel type. This speeds up an initial assessment.

Can leaning posts on a slope be re-set?expand_more

Often yes, if the timber is sound at ground level. Posts on sloping gardens in suburbs such as Harborne and Kings Heath may need deeper footings in concrete to resist soil movement.
