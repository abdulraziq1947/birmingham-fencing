Commercial Fencing Birmingham

Home/Commercial Fencing Birmingham

Commercial fencing defines the perimeter of business yards, storage compounds, schools and light industrial units across Birmingham's industrial and residential districts and modern trading estates. Specification depends on security requirement, visibility, and whether the boundary adjoins public footpaths or neighbouring residential plots.

Systems used on commercial sites

Mesh panel fencing, palisade and timber acoustic or screen fencing each suit different sites. We describe the proposed system, post footing and any gated access in the quote.

Higher security requirements may need security fencing specification. Residential-style garden fencing may suit boundaries adjoining housing.

Areas we work in

Commercial fencing is quoted across Birmingham including Aston, Witton, Erdington and Stechford. See areas we cover for postcodes served.
