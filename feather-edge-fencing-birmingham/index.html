Feather Edge Fencing Birmingham

Home/Feather Edge Fencing Birmingham

Feather edge fencing uses tapered boards that overlap along the run. It can be built on site between posts or installed as prefabricated panels where bay lengths suit standard sizes on level rear gardens across Birmingham's suburbs.

When feather edge suits the site

Level rear gardens with standard bay lengths suit panel installation. Irregular boundaries or slopes on clay-heavy suburban plots may need on-site boarding. We confirm method in the quote.

Frequently asked questions

Are feather edge panels standard width?expand_more

Common panel widths are 1.8 m or 2.4 m between posts. Non-standard lengths require on-site construction or adjusted post centres.

Is feather edge cheaper than closeboard?expand_more

Feather edge is often lower cost than closeboard because boards overlap rather than meet flush. Privacy is slightly less complete than a solid closeboard face.

Can you repair feather edge panels?expand_more

Yes — individual panels or boards can be replaced where posts remain sound. See our fence repairs page for storm and rot damage.

Does feather edge work on sloping gardens?expand_more

Slopes may need on-site overlap boarding rather than prefabricated panels. We assess ground level in suburbs such as Moseley and Kings Heath when quoting.
