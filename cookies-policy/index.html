Home/Cookies Policy

Last updated: 10 June 2026

This cookies policy explains how Fencing Company Birmingham - Installations and Repairs Contractor uses cookies and similar technologies on fencingcompanybirmingham.co.uk.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites function, remember preferences, or understand how visitors use a service. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels used by embedded content.

How we use cookies

Our website is a static information site with an embedded quote form and map. We do not currently use first-party analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics) on this site. Cookies you encounter are mainly from embedded third-party services.

Types of cookies on this site

Strictly necessary / functional

These support core features such as loading embedded content. They may be set by third parties when you interact with their embeds:

  • Tally (quote form) — when you use the “Request a free fencing quote” form, Tally may set cookies to operate the form, prevent abuse and maintain session state. See Tally’s cookie information.

Third-party content cookies

  • Google Maps — our contact page embeds Google Maps, which may set cookies to display the map and remember preferences. See Google’s cookie policy.
  • Google Fonts — typography is loaded from Google servers; Google may log IP address and related technical data. See Google’s privacy and cookie documentation.

Marketing cookies

We do not intentionally place advertising or social media tracking cookies on this website.

Cookie duration

Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain until they expire or you delete them — duration depends on the third-party provider (often from days to two years).

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Block all or third-party cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Browse in private/incognito mode

Blocking cookies may affect how the quote form or map displays. Browser help:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Do Not Track

We do not respond separately to Do Not Track browser signals because there is no consistent industry standard. You can use browser cookie controls instead.

Personal data and cookies

Information collected via cookies may constitute personal data. Our Privacy Policy and GDPR Policy explain how we handle personal data and your rights.

Changes

If we add analytics, advertising or other cookies in future, we will update this page and, where required by law, obtain consent before non-essential cookies are placed.
